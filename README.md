# MediTrack

## Smart Hospital Management System

MediTrack is an integrated hospital management platform designed to
connect patients, doctors, reception staff, and administrators in one
digital system.

## Problem Statement

Hospitals often rely on multiple disconnected systems to manage
patient records, appointments, medicines, emergency services,
doctor schedules, queues, and ambulance operations. This can cause
delays, inefficient coordination, and difficulty accessing important
information.

MediTrack aims to provide a unified platform that improves hospital
workflow, information accessibility, and patient experience.

## Key Features

- Patient Management
- Doctor Management
- Appointment Scheduling
- Medical Records
- Medicine Management
- Emergency Services
- Ambulance Tracking
- Queue Management
- Hospital Dashboard
- AI Health Assistant
